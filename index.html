Link Google Calendar

  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com. On the left, hover the calendar you want, tap the ⋮ → Settings and sharing.
  2. Scroll down to Integrate calendar and copy the Calendar ID (it looks like an email address). Copying the "Public URL" or embed code works too.
  3. Paste it below and tap Link It.

💡 Private calendars only show if the tablet's browser is signed in to that Google account. Otherwise, turn on "Make available to public" on the same settings page (fine for a Classroom calendar, think twice for a personal one).

✨ Combine Google Calendar

This pulls the family calendar's events right into the dashboard calendar — dots on the days, the day card, Today's Brief, and This Week. It needs a one-time setup in Google (a parent does this once on a computer; the steps are in the Setup Guide under "Combining Google Calendar"). At the end you get a web address that starts with https://script.google.com/ — paste it here.
